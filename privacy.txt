<h1>StrikeSet Privacy Policy</h1>
<p class="updated">Last updated: July 24, 2026</p>

<p>
  This Privacy Policy explains how StrikeSet ("we", "us", "our") collects, uses, and
  protects your information when you use the StrikeSet mobile application (the "App").
  By using StrikeSet, you agree to the collection and use of information as described
  in this policy.
</p>

<h2>1. Information We Collect</h2>

<p>We collect only the information needed to run the App's core features:</p>

<ul>
  <li>
    <strong>Account information</strong> — your email address and password. Your password
    is handled by our authentication provider (Supabase Auth) and stored in hashed form;
    we never see or store it in plain text.
  </li>
  <li>
    <strong>Profile information</strong> — your display name, username, and the character
    avatar you choose.
  </li>
  <li>
    <strong>Activity data</strong> — your points, streaks, completed workouts, and any
    custom workout lists you create.
  </li>
  <li>
    <strong>Social data</strong> — your friends list, friend requests, and duel (challenge)
    history with other users.
  </li>
  <li>
    <strong>Device information</strong> — a push-notification token used solely to deliver
    notifications to your device (e.g. duel invites, daily reminders).
  </li>
</ul>

<p>
  We do <strong>not</strong> collect your location, access your camera or contacts, collect
  payment or financial information, or use any advertising SDKs.
</p>

<h2>2. How We Use Your Information</h2>

<ul>
  <li>To create and secure your account, and let you sign in.</li>
  <li>To provide the App's social and competitive features — friends, duels, and leaderboards.</li>
  <li>To send push notifications you've enabled (duel invites, friend requests, workout reminders).</li>
  <li>To track your progress — points, streaks, and workout history.</li>
</ul>

<h2>3. How We Share Your Information</h2>

<p>
  We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it with advertisers.
  We use the following third-party service providers to operate the App:
</p>

<ul>
  <li>
    <strong>Supabase</strong> — provides our backend database and authentication. Your
    account, profile, and activity data are stored on Supabase's infrastructure.
  </li>
  <li>
    <strong>Google Firebase Cloud Messaging</strong> — delivers push notifications to your
    device. Firebase receives only the device token necessary to deliver a notification.
  </li>
</ul>

<p>
  Other users of the App can see information you choose to share by using social features —
  for example, your display name, username, avatar, points, and streak are visible to your
  friends and on leaderboards.
</p>

<h2>4. Data Security</h2>

<p>
  All data is transmitted over encrypted (HTTPS) connections. Access to your data in our
  database is restricted using row-level security policies, so other users' accounts cannot
  read or modify your data directly.
</p>

<h2>5. Data Retention</h2>

<p>
  We retain your information for as long as your account is active. If you delete your
  account, your data is permanently and immediately deleted — see below.
</p>

<h2>6. Your Rights and Choices</h2>

<ul>
  <li>
    <strong>Delete your account</strong> — you can permanently delete your account and all
    associated data at any time from within the App (Profile → Settings → Delete Account).
    This action is immediate and cannot be undone.
  </li>
  <li>
    <strong>Access or update your information</strong> — you can view and update your profile
    information directly within the App.
  </li>
  <li>
    <strong>Notifications</strong> — you can disable push notifications at any time from
    Profile → Settings → Notifications.
  </li>
</ul>

<h2>7. Children's Privacy</h2>

<p>
  StrikeSet is not directed at children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect
  personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with
  personal information, please contact us and we will delete it.
</p>

<h2>8. Changes to This Policy</h2>

<p>
  We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this
  page with an updated "Last updated" date. Continued use of the App after changes take
  effect constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
</p>

<h2>9. Contact Us</h2>

<p>
  If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or how your data is handled, contact
  us at <a href="mailto:strikesetapp@gmail.com">strikesetapp@gmail.com</a>.
</p>

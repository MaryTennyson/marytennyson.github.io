About us

Small team. Real products.

Embaxus is a student-led, independent app development initiative. Our products are built voluntarily alongside university studies, with a focus on learning, experimentation and creating software that people can actually use.

We are starting with mobile applications and currently operate through our developer account, Embaxus House, on Google Play. As the project grows, the goal is to turn promising ideas into polished products and keep building from there.

“Build it, learn from it, make it better.”

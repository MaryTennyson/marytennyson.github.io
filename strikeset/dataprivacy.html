Data & Privacy

Version 1 Effective August 14, 2026 Applies to StrikeSet (Android)

This page provides a plain-language summary of how StrikeSet collects, uses, and protects your information. For the complete legal details, please see our Privacy Policy .

What we collect, and why

  • Email address & password — Your email address is used to create and manage your account, sign in, reset your password, and send necessary account-related emails. Your password is not stored in plain text and is handled through Supabase Auth using cryptographic password hashing.
  • Username, display name, date of birth & avatar — Your username and display name identify you within the App. Your date of birth is used to verify that you meet the minimum age requirement and is not displayed to other users. Your avatar is a preset character and color selected within the App; we do not require or collect a personal photo.
  • Workout lists, workout history, points & streaks — This information allows StrikeSet to provide its core workout, progress-tracking, streak, and scoring features.
  • Duels, friends, friend requests & trophies — This information is used to provide StrikeSet's social features, including friend connections, workout duels, leaderboards, and achievements.
  • Device timezone — Your device timezone is used to determine your local day so that daily missions and streaks reset according to your local midnight.
  • Push notification token — A Firebase Cloud Messaging installation identifier allows StrikeSet to deliver notifications such as duel invitations, friend activity, and workout reminders.
  • Crash and diagnostic data — Firebase Crashlytics may collect information such as your device model, operating system version, App version, and crash information to help us identify and fix technical problems. This information is associated with an installation identifier rather than your name or email address.

What we don't collect

StrikeSet does not collect your precise location, contacts, photos, microphone or camera data, or browsing history. We do not use advertising or third-party advertising SDKs, and we do not sell or rent your personal information.

Who processes your data

StrikeSet relies on a limited number of infrastructure providers to operate its core services:

  • Supabase — provides backend infrastructure, database services, authentication, and real-time data synchronization for StrikeSet.
  • Firebase / Google — Firebase Cloud Messaging is used to deliver push notifications and Firebase Crashlytics is used for crash reporting and diagnostics. We do not use Firebase Analytics, Firestore, or other Firebase or Google products for advertising or analytics.

These providers process information on our behalf as necessary to provide their services to StrikeSet. For additional information about how your information is handled, including applicable provider privacy practices, please see our Privacy Policy .

What you control

  • Edit your information — You can view and update your profile information through Settings → Edit Profile.
  • Manage notifications — You can disable push notifications at any time through the App's settings or your device settings.
  • Delete your account — You can permanently delete your account through Settings → Delete Account. Account deletion removes your profile and associated App data, including workout data, duel history, and friend connections, subject to the retention practices described in our Privacy Policy.
  • Privacy requests — You may contact us regarding data access, correction, export, or other privacy-related requests using the contact information below.

Children's privacy

StrikeSet is not directed to children under 13, and users must confirm that they are at least 13 years old when creating an account. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

If you believe that a child under 13 has created an account or provided personal information through StrikeSet, please contact us so that we can review the situation and take appropriate action.

Questions about your data or privacy? Email us at strikesetapp@gmail.com .

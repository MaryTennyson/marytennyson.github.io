By creating an account or using StrikeSet, you agree to the collection and use of information as described here.

2. How your password is protected

Your password is never stored in plain text. Authentication is handled by Supabase Auth, which stores only a salted cryptographic hash — we, and Supabase, cannot see or recover your actual password. The App may separately keep a salted fingerprint on your device only to avoid re-prompting your phone's password manager to save a credential it has already saved; this fingerprint never leaves your device and cannot be reversed into your password.

3. How we use your information

  • Create and secure your account
  • Operate core features: daily missions, streaks, duels, friends, leaderboards, trophies
  • Send push notifications you'd expect — you can turn these off in Settings
  • Diagnose crashes and fix bugs
  • Enforce our Terms of Service and keep the App fair, e.g. preventing manipulated scores
  • Comply with legal obligations

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use it for third-party advertising.

4. Who we share information with

We use a small number of infrastructure providers to run StrikeSet. We do not sell or rent your data, and never share it for advertising.

Supabase — backend, database, authentication

StrikeSet's account system, database, and real-time sync run on Supabase. Your account and app data are processed on Supabase's infrastructure, primarily hosted in the United States. Supabase is SOC 2 Type II and ISO 27001 certified, and acts as our data processor — it only processes your data on our instructions.

Supabase's privacy practices: supabase.com/privacy

Firebase / Google — push notifications & crash reporting

We use Firebase Cloud Messaging to deliver push notifications and Firebase Crashlytics to catch and fix crashes. We do not use Firebase Analytics, Firestore, or any other Firebase/Google product.

Firebase privacy practices: firebase.google.com/support/privacy · Data Processing Terms: firebase.google.com/terms/data-processing-terms

Other users

Your username, display name, avatar, streak, points, and duel/leaderboard results are visible to your friends inside the App by design — this is a social feature, not a data leak. Your email and password are never shown to other users.

Legal

We may disclose information if required by law, or to protect the rights, safety, or property of Embaxus, our users, or the public.

5. Data retention

We keep your account data for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account (Settings → Delete Account), we permanently delete your profile, workout data, duel history, and friend connections. Crash diagnostics collected by Firebase Crashlytics are automatically deleted from Google's systems within 90 days of collection.

6. Your rights & choices

  • Access & correction — view and edit your profile directly in the App (Settings → Edit Profile)
  • Delete your account — Settings → Delete Account permanently removes your account and associated data
  • Push notifications — turn off anytime in Settings
  • Data export or other privacy requests — contact us at the email below

If you are in the European Economic Area, UK, or a similar jurisdiction, you may also have rights to restrict or object to processing, and to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

7. Children's privacy

StrikeSet is not directed at children under 13, and we require users to confirm they are at least 13 at sign-up. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has provided us with personal information, contact us so we can delete it.

8. International data transfers

Because our infrastructure providers (Supabase, Google Firebase) operate globally, your information may be processed in countries other than your own, including the United States. Where required, these providers rely on mechanisms such as Standard Contractual Clauses or the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework to lawfully transfer personal data internationally.

9. Security

We use encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS) between the App and our servers, database-level access controls (Row Level Security) so users can only reach data they're authorized to see, and salted password hashing. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, but we work to protect your information appropriately.

If we make material changes, we'll ask you to re-review and accept the updated policy the next time you open the App — you'll see the version number and date at the top of this page change. Continued use of the App after a change constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.

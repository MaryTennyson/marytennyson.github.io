Delete Your Account

Applies to StrikeSet (Android)

You can permanently delete your StrikeSet account and all associated data at any time. There are two ways to do it, depending on whether you still have the app installed.

Option 1 — From inside the app (instant)

1.Open StrikeSet and go to Profile → Settings.
2.Tap Delete Account, enter your password to confirm, and confirm again.
3.Your account and data are deleted immediately — there's no waiting period.

Option 2 — Without the app (by email)

If you no longer have the app installed, or can't sign in, email us at strikesetapp@gmail.com from the email address your account is registered under, with the subject line "Delete my account". We'll verify it's you and process the deletion within 30 days.

What gets deleted

  • Your account (email, username, display name, date of birth)
  • Your workout list and daily completion history
  • Streaks, points, and skip-pass history
  • Duel history and friend connections
  • Trophies earned

This is permanent and can't be undone. Deleting your account does not delete you from other users' view retroactively for actions already taken (e.g. a duel result that already completed) — but your profile and future visibility are removed.

What isn't deleted immediately

Anonymous crash diagnostics collected via Firebase Crashlytics aren't tied to your account or identity, and aren't deleted by an account-deletion request — they're automatically removed from Google's systems within 90 days of collection regardless, per our Privacy Policy.

Questions about account deletion? Email us at strikesetapp@gmail.com. See also our Privacy Policy and Data & Privacy pages.

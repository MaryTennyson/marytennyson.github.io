Founding Business Partner

Help shape StrikeSet's business, product, and future from an early stage.

We are looking for someone who wants to become part of the founding team and help shape StrikeSet from an early stage. You will work closely with the existing team on business, product, and strategic decisions. This is not an administrative assistant position. We want a genuine founding team member who takes part in meaningful decisions about StrikeSet's business, product direction, growth, and future.

What you'll do

  • Help StrikeSet establish and manage its presence on both the Google Play Store and Apple App Store.
  • Help with the publishing and account side of the app under Embedded House, as appropriate.
  • Research and understand the business, administrative, and operational requirements of running a mobile app.
  • Take part in important business and product decisions.
  • Help evaluate opportunities for StrikeSet's growth.
  • Research topics the team needs to understand and turn that research into practical decisions.
  • Contribute ideas about the future direction of StrikeSet.
  • Help with business-related responsibilities as the project grows.
  • If StrikeSet begins generating revenue, responsibly take part in the related business and financial processes, including understanding applicable tax responsibilities and seeking professional guidance when necessary.

Who we're looking for

You may have a background or strong interest in business, entrepreneurship, technology, computer science, software, product development, business operations, or a related field. A specific degree is not required. More important than your education, we are looking for someone who is:

  • Curious and willing to learn.
  • Comfortable researching unfamiliar topics.
  • Proactive and able to take initiative.
  • Resourceful and good at figuring things out.
  • Responsible and trustworthy.
  • Comfortable communicating with people.
  • Friendly, cooperative, and easy to work with.
  • Comfortable sharing ideas and opinions, and able to disagree respectfully.
  • Interested in being part of a real founding team rather than simply completing assigned tasks.

Compensation

We cannot offer a fixed salary at this stage. This is an early-stage founding opportunity for someone who wants to help build StrikeSet from the ground up and gain meaningful startup experience. If StrikeSet generates revenue in the future, compensation and/or revenue sharing can be discussed and structured fairly according to each founding member's responsibilities and contribution.

Additional benefits

Mobile App Developer (iOS / Android)

Our main need right now: an iOS developer to bring StrikeSet to iOS.

StrikeSet already has an Android developer on the team, and our current primary need is an iOS developer who can help bring the existing app to iOS. The Android app is the reference for the functionality and user experience to bring to the iOS platform.

What you'll do

  • Develop the iOS version of StrikeSet.
  • Translate the existing Android functionality and app logic into an appropriate iOS implementation.
  • Work with the Android developer to keep both platforms consistent.
  • Help identify and solve technical issues.
  • Contribute to the quality and usability of the app.
  • Discuss technical and product decisions with the team.
  • Help improve the app as new features are developed.

Who we're looking for

We are open to experienced iOS developers, developers who are currently learning iOS, computer science and software engineering students, and self-taught developers who can show their ability and willingness to learn. You do not need to know everything already. We value:

  • Willingness to learn and curiosity.
  • Problem-solving skills and the ability to research independently.
  • Interest in mobile development.
  • Attention to detail.
  • Communication and collaboration.
  • Willingness to improve the app rather than simply reproduce code.

Team support

Our Android developer can help with existing app functionality, feature requirements, how features are expected to behave, general app architecture and logic, and questions about the Android implementation. Claude Code can also be provided as a development and research assistant.

Hardware

A Mac is required. We currently cannot provide one, so candidates should have access to a Mac capable of supporting their iOS development workflow.

Time commitment

Flexible hours. This does not need to be a full-time position. You can contribute around your school, work, or other commitments.

Android developers

Although our primary need is iOS, Android developers who want to contribute to StrikeSet on a volunteer, early-stage basis are also welcome to contact us.

Early access

Want to understand the app before committing? You can request early access to StrikeSet.

Compensation

There is currently no fixed salary for this early-stage position. If StrikeSet generates revenue in the future, compensation and/or revenue sharing can be discussed based on contribution and responsibilities.

Fitness Specialist

Help us improve the exercise and workout content inside StrikeSet.

We are looking for someone with fitness knowledge who can help us improve the exercise and workout content inside StrikeSet. This position intentionally requires significantly less time than our other roles.

What you'll do

  • Review the exercises currently included in StrikeSet.
  • Suggest useful exercises that could be added.
  • Give feedback on how exercises should be organized.
  • Suggest improvements to exercise descriptions and instructions.
  • Identify areas where the workout experience could be improved.
  • Give feedback on whether exercises make sense for different workout goals.
  • Suggest improvements to the fitness-related parts of the app.
  • Help review and make appropriate adjustments to exercise content.

Who we're looking for

A good fit could be someone with fitness experience, personal training experience, exercise science knowledge, strength training knowledge, sports or fitness education, or strong practical knowledge of exercise and workouts. We value someone who:

  • Gives constructive feedback.
  • Is willing to research when necessary.
  • Communicates ideas clearly.
  • Understands that the goal is to make StrikeSet more useful for real users.
  • Is interested in contributing to an early-stage fitness technology project.

Time commitment

Significantly less time than the other positions. This is a flexible contributor or advisor-style role, not a daily commitment.

Early access

You can request early access to StrikeSet to explore the app and understand the current exercise and workout system before giving feedback.
